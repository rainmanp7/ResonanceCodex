The Temperature Ceiling — This is Solvable
The 700–800°C amorphous-to-crystalline phase shift is the single biggest risk. At lab scale you manage it with shallow trays and restricted airflow. At manufacturing scale, the standard solutions are:
Rotary drum kilns with inline thermocouples — continuous feed, controlled atmosphere, temperature held in the 550–650°C sweet spot
Fluidized bed calcination — the ash is suspended in an airflow, which distributes heat evenly and prevents hot-spot accumulation; this is the most common industrial method for exactly this type of silica
Flash calcination — very short residence time at higher temperature; the material passes through quickly enough that it doesn't dwell in the crystallization zone
